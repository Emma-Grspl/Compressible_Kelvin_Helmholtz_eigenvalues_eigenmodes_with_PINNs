PHASE 5 — CLAIM CONSOLIDATION

This phase did not correspond to an additional numerical experiment.

Its purpose was to rewrite and consolidate the scientific claims after
Phases 1–4, in particular:

- physics residuals establish physical/differential consistency;
- the anchors-only ablation does not establish a causal branch-selection
  advantage from the physics losses;
- sparse spectral anchors dominate direct spectral branch localization;
- classical correction remains responsible for the final high-accuracy
  eigenvalue.

No standalone numerical output is therefore associated with Phase 5.
