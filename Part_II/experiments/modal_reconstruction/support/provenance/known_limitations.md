# Known limitations

- The raw confirmed modal fields are the primary scientific reference.
- The tail-polished fields are a derived export.
- Tail polishing does not modify `cr` or `ci`.
- Weak oscillatory tails remain sensitive for selected points.
- Five spectral points retain an explicit boundary flag.
- Digitized Blumen points are discrete observations.
- Blumen points must not be connected by plotting lines.
- The high-Mach M=1.8 and M=1.9 modal cores are stable, while weak tails remain
  more sensitive to the spatial mapping.
